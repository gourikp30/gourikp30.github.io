Level 1 High-Power Rocket

Rose Rocketry, Rose-Hulman, Fall 2026

  • Rose Rocketry
  • OpenRocket
  • H135 motor
  • L1 Certified

As a member of Rose Rocketry, I built and flew a high-power rocket to earn my Level 1 certification from the National Association of Rocketry.

I assembled the rocket myself, including the booster tube, payload tube and coupler, and integrated the recovery deployment system.

Before launch, I modeled and simulated the flight in OpenRocket on an H135 motor, which predicted an apogee of 1,927 ft. The rocket flew and was recovered successfully, and I earned my L1 certification.

Farmer's Sense Smart Probe & App

Technology Student Association Engineering Design Challenge, 2025

  • Team Captain
  • Onshape
  • 1st, Virginia State
  • 5th, TSA Nationals

For the 2025 Technology Student Association Engineering Design Challenge, my team and I developed Farmer's Sense, a response to the prompt “Manage the Nitrogen Cycle”. Farmer's Sense won 1st at the Virginia State Level Competition, and 5th at the National Conference.

What is Farmer's Sense?

Farmer's Sense is an in-soil sensor used to provide real-time feedback to farmers about their soil to prevent improper farming practices that cause incomplete nitrification. This is important because incomplete nitrification can lead to decreased crop yield, and harmful intermediate compounds in the soil (Nitrite). The Farmer's Sense Smart Probe monitors factors that impact incomplete nitrification the most, including pH, temperature, oxygen, electrical conductivity, and water moisture. The Farmer's Sense Smart Probe then sends the sensor information to the Farmer's Sense app, allowing the farmer to make decisions about maintaining their land through the use of real-time, accurate data. This can help prevent poor agricultural practices such as over-tilling, over-fertilizing, and poor management of irrigation systems which contribute to incomplete nitrification.

What did I do?

During this challenge, I conducted research about factors that impact incomplete nitrification, and existing methods to measure those factors. I then modeled the concept for the Smart Probe using Onshape.

I was also the captain of this 5-person team, managing the design process from research and development through the presentation and pitching of the concept to competition judges. Due to the nature of the competition and limited resources, Farmer's Sense was not fully prototyped or turned into a usable product.

Fine Motor Miko

Design & Communication Studio I, Rose-Hulman, 2026

  • Client: REACH
  • PLA & TPU 3D printing
  • Prototype 3

Fine Motor Miko is a doll that teaches children important daily fine motor skills such as tying hair, shoe laces, fastening buttons and zippers. This project started in my Studio I class, after talking to REACH services, who expressed their need for tools that teach these daily fine motor skills.

This is Prototype 3 of Miko, who is a soft body doll with a PLA internal skeleton, ball and socket joints, and stuffed TPU limbs. The fine motor aspect includes modifications to already existing fasteners, such as longer tags for easier zipper pulling, and thicker, more visible laces. She was specifically designed to meet certain needs that REACH expressed, such as easy accessibility, durability and hygiene. After our recent presentation to REACH, they expressed more needs, such as the ability to sit, which will be tackled in Studio II.
